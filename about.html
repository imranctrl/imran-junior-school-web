About Imran Junior School

Discover our rich history, visionary leadership, mission, and unwavering core values.

Our Background & Journey

Established in 2023, Imran Junior School & Quran Memorisation Centre is a premier Islamic-founded educational institution situated along the serene Luteete-Gayaza Road, Kampala. Founded under the visionary and dedicated leadership of Director Hajji Kasim Seremba and Hajjat Sharifah (Holder of a Bachelor of Arts in Languages and Islamic Studies), the institution began its noble journey accommodating Nursery up to Primary Four with a modest yet ambitious pioneer cohort of just 19 pupils.

Through rigorous discipline, unyielding academic commitment, and spiritual dedication, our pioneer candidates successfully sat for their IPLE (Islamic Primary Leaving Examinations) in the year 2025, registering magnificent results that stunned the community. Building upon this solid foundation, the school proudly presents candidates for both IPLE and PLE, expanding its horizons to shape a brighter generation.

We combine modern national curriculum competencies with deep-rooted theological studies and specialized Tahfidh Quran memorisation, ensuring that every child achieves absolute balance between worldly intelligence and spiritual enlightenment.
