Dual Curriculum Structure

Balanced Intellectual & Spiritual Nurturing

At Imran Junior School, education goes beyond standard classroom learning. We run a dual-track academic framework: the Uganda National Primary School Curriculum alongside a comprehensive Islamic Theology & Tahfidh Quran Memorisation System.

Secular Primary Education

Designed around the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) syllabus, focusing on critical thinking, problem-solving, literacy, and scientific inquiry.

  • Mathematics: Practical numeracy, logic, and analytical problem-solving.
  • Integrated Science: Hands-on experiments, health education, and environmental studies.
  • English Language: Grammar, composition, speech, and public reading skills.
  • Social Studies (SST): Geography, history, civics, and community responsibilities.
  • ICT & Physical Education: Digital literacy and physical fitness.

IPLE & PLE Track Record

In 2025, our 19 pioneer pupils sat for IPLE with remarkable distinctions. By 2026/2027, our candidates sit for both IPLE and PLE, establishing a double qualification pathway.

Enroll for 2027

Tahfidh & Islamic Theology Centre

Supervised by qualified Sheikhs and tutors, under the leadership of Hajjat Sharifah (BA in Languages & Islamic Studies). Students complete structured Quran memorisation alongside Islamic ethics.

  • Hifz (Quran Memorisation): Daily structured memorisation sessions tailored to child capability.
  • Tajweed: Proper articulation, rules, and melodic recitation.
  • Fiqh & Ibadaat: Practical Islamic jurisprudence, prayer (Salah), and purification.
  • Hadith & Tarbiya: Prophetic traditions, character building, and respect.
  • Arabic Language: Reading, writing, and understanding Quranic Arabic.

Tahfidh Boarding & Day Tracks

Pupils enrolled in the Tahfidh program enjoy dedicated morning and evening memorisation hours with personalized progress tracking for each Juz.
